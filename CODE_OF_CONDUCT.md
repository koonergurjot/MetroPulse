# Code of Conduct

MetroPulse follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This is a short version of what that means here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good faith and give specific, constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who rely on it.
- Credit other people's work.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would
  reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate or harmful.

To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form and mark it as a conduct concern, or
contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile
(<https://github.com/koonergurjot>). Reports are handled promptly and
confidentially.
